# Indian Railways Operating Rules: Signaling and Safety Headway Intervals

## HEAD-01: Automatic Block Signaling Minimum Separation (4-Aspect)
On sections equipped with continuous 4-aspect automatic block signaling (such as Mathura-Kota-Ratlam), a minimum headway separation of 7 minutes or two clear signal blocks must be preserved between successive following trains traveling in the same direction.

## HEAD-02: Intermediate Block Post (IBP) Minimum Headway
In semi-automatic and IBP controlled territory, no train shall be allowed to enter the block section between the station starter and the IBP signal until the preceding train has completely passed the IBP home signal and the track circuit has proven unoccupied for at least 10 minutes.

## HEAD-03: Absolute Block Working Minimum Time Interval
Under Absolute Block signaling working, only one train is permitted in the entire inter-station block section at any given moment. A minimum dispatch spacing of 15 minutes must be enforced between successive departure permissions at preceding junction stations.

## HEAD-04: Foggy Weather Block Margins and Visibility Test Object
During dense fog when the Visibility Test Object (VTO) is not clearly distinguishable from the station master office at a distance of 180 meters, maximum train speed is restricted to 60 km/h (or 75 km/h if equipped with Kavach/FOG-PILOT). Scheduled headway intervals must be expanded by an additional 5 minutes at all diverging junctions.

## HEAD-05: Following Train Distance on Caution Aspect
When approaching a signal displaying a Yellow (Caution) aspect, loco pilots must decelerate to 30 km/h and be prepared to stop at the next signal. Controllers must not permit dispatch of a following train until the leading train has reported clearance of the cautionary turnout block.
