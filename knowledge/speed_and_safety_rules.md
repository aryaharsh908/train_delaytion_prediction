# Indian Railways Operating Rules: Speed Restrictions and Safety Protocols

## SPED-01: Maximum Permissible Speed (MPS) on Delhi-Mumbai Corridor
The standard Maximum Permissible Speed (MPS) on Delhi-Mathura-Kota-Ratlam-Mumbai trunk route is 130 km/h for LHB rake passenger trains and 160 km/h for certified Kavach-compliant Vande Bharat services. Freight trains are restricted to 75 km/h (loaded) and 100 km/h (empty/container).

## SPED-02: Temporary Speed Restriction (TSR) Caution Orders
Section Controllers must issue written and digital Caution Orders (Form T/409) for any section where track defects, ballast replacement, or deep screening are active. Speeds mandated by TSR (typically 30 km/h, 45 km/h, or 75 km/h) must be factored into dynamic ETA calculations.

## SPED-03: Turnout and Crossover Speed Restrictions
Movement over 1-in-8.5 and 1-in-12 turnouts from main line to loop line is restricted to 15 km/h and 30 km/h respectively, unless thick-web switches with curved switches are installed, permitting 50 km/h.

## SPED-04: Neutral Section Gliding and OHE Tripping
Loco pilots must open main circuit breakers (DJ/VCB) 500 meters before entering an Overhead Equipment (OHE) neutral section to prevent arc flashes. Section Controllers must not schedule heavy acceleration or starts immediately preceding a neutral section on steep gradients.

## SPED-05: Hot Axle Box and Flat Tyre Emergency Stop
If an acoustic bearing monitor, wheel impact load detector (WILD), or trackside station staff reports smoke, hot axle, or irregular thumping indicative of a flat tyre exceeding 50 mm, the train must be stopped immediately at the nearest block signal or station loop for safety inspection.
