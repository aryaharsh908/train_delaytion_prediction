# Indian Railways Operating Rules: Train Precedence and Merging Hierarchy

## PREC-01: Vande Bharat and Rajdhani Express Absolute Precedence
Vande Bharat Express, Rajdhani Express, and Tejas Superfast services hold absolute operational precedence over all other passenger, parcel, and freight trains on Western Trunk Corridor merging junctions. When converging within a 25-kilometer headway window, Section Controllers must regulate lower-precedence services onto loop lines or preceding block sections to ensure unimpeded through-clearance.

## PREC-02: Shatabdi and Duronto Express High-Speed Priority
Shatabdi and Duronto Express trains occupy Tier-1 precedence immediately following Vande Bharat/Rajdhani. No freight or passenger train may be dispatched into an adjacent block section if it would compel a Tier-1 express to receive a double-yellow or yellow cautionary aspect within 12 minutes of estimated junction arrival.

## PREC-03: Mail / Express Precedence Over Freight Services
Scheduled Mail and Express trains have precedence over standard goods, empty rake, and container trains. Goods trains must be detained or diverted to station passing loops if a following Mail/Express is trailing within 18 kilometers, unless the goods train can clear the section with a minimum 8-minute margin.

## PREC-04: Time-Tabled Container and RO-RO Freight Precedence
Scheduled Automobile and Container Freight Corridor (CONCOR) priority trains maintain precedence over empty rake transfers and unscheduled departmental material trains. At converging junctions, empty wagons must yield to loaded priority container trains.

## PREC-05: Accident Relief Medical Van (ARMV) Absolute Corridor Priority
Accident Relief Medical Vans (ARMV) and Breakdown Cranes responding to emergency safety dispatches possess supreme absolute corridor precedence over all trains, including Rajdhani and Vande Bharat. All active signals must be configured to green wave status until the relief train passes the designated division boundary.
