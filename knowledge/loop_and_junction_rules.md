# Indian Railways Operating Rules: Loop Regulation and Junction Protocols

## LOOP-01: Main Line Through Run Preference
High-speed passenger trains scheduled to run non-stop through a junction station must be routed strictly on the main through line. Diversion to a loop line is strictly prohibited unless emergency speed restrictions or mechanical track obstructions prevent main line transit.

## LOOP-02: Loop Line Overtake Protocol (18-km Rule)
When a freight or stopping passenger train is operating ahead of a high-priority Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, or Mail/Express train, the Section Controller must arrange for the lower-priority train to be looped at the nearest station having a clear siding once the headway drops below 18 kilometers.

## LOOP-03: Simultaneous Reception Restrictions
Simultaneous reception of two converging trains on adjacent station loops is prohibited unless the station layout includes an approved sand hump or overrun buffer stop providing at least 120 meters of safety isolation past the starter signal.

## LOOP-04: Station Platform Clearing Margin
A minimum platform clearance buffer of 4 minutes must elapse between the departure of a scheduled train and the setting of signal route interlocking for an incoming train assigned to the same platform road.

## LOOP-05: Catch Siding and Slip Siding Routing
In falling gradient sections exceeding 1 in 100, trains held at home signals must be set towards catch sidings until the train has come to a complete standstill and route setting is confirmed by the station panel interlock.
